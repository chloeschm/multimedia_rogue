# First full flutter/flame game !!

#### Mobile Buttons:
Fire = Attack
Joystick left side for movement
Settings/Pause on top right

#### Web Buttons:
Space = Attack
WASD for movement
Settings/Pause on top right

## First World: Pencil
These little scribble slimes will be the easiest you’ll encounter. Chasing you around just when you get near, these guys only take one extra-wide swing of your pencil to kill. You’ve just got to find the randomized enemy holding your next weapon to unlock the entrance to the next world.

### Pencil Weapon: 
Your first weapon you spawn in with. This has a WIDE swing but doesn’t do any extra damage. 

## Second World: Pen
There are a few more enemies in this room, and three at a time will chase you constantly, but you still only need to kill the randomized enemy holding your next weapon upgrade to move on.

### Pen Weapon:
Your first weapon upgrade, and also the only ranged weapon in the game. Shoot ink blots (only horizontally) to kill. Unlimited fire rate and bullets at your disposal. 

## Third World: Marker
Honestly the real challenge starts here. Randomized movement, more of them, and now they’re faster. Think carefully as you go about looking for your next weapon upgrade. 

### Marker Weapon:
The most unique weapon in the game; leaves a trail of dotted markers behind as you hold the fire button. Enemies will die as they run into them, but this will also remove the trail they die from.

Fourth World: Watercolor
This is what your training has all led up to. As this is currently the finale, you’ll have to kill all enemies to complete this world. More randomized movement, but they’re slower than the marker tips. Don’t take this as a good sign though, as behind their trail they leave paint blots that will slow you down if you step on them. More health than any other enemy, so be sure to save up your HP for your final battle. 

### Watercolor Brush:
Shorter ranged melee than the pencil, but it will do more damage.
